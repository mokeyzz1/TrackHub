# TrackHub Marketing Strategy

## Overview

TrackHub is a free college track & field app. The goal is to become the go-to app for tracking college track performances.

**Target Users:**
- College track athletes (primary)
- Track coaches
- Track fans and parents
- High school athletes interested in college track

**Value Proposition:**
Clean, fast access to college track data with World Athletics scoring for cross-event comparison.

---

## Phase 1: Seed Users (Week 1-2)

### Goal
Get 50-100 initial users from personal network.

### Actions
1. **Personal network**
   - Text teammates and track friends directly
   - Share in any track group chats
   - Ask 10 people to download and leave App Store reviews

2. **Instagram story**
   - Post story announcing the app
   - "I built this, check it out"
   - Link to App Store

3. **Gather feedback**
   - Ask early users what's missing
   - Note any bugs or issues
   - This shapes your messaging for broader launch

### Success Metrics
- 50+ downloads
- 5+ App Store reviews
- Feedback collected

---

## Phase 2: Content Engine (Week 2+, Ongoing)

### Goal
Build a following by providing value through track content.

### Actions
1. **Create TrackHub Instagram account**
   - Handle: @trackhubapp (or similar)
   - Bio: "College track & field stats, PRs, and rankings. Free app link below."
   - Link to App Store

2. **Post schedule (3-5x per week)**
   - Monday: Top performers from last week
   - Wednesday: Head-to-head comparison (tag both athletes)
   - Friday: PR alerts or school spotlight
   - Weekend: Engagement post (poll, question)

3. **Content that gets shared**
   - Tag athletes when you feature them
   - Tag school accounts
   - Athletes reshare = free exposure to their followers

4. **TikTok (optional but high potential)**
   - Short videos showing app features
   - "How to find your WA score"
   - "Comparing [Athlete] vs [Athlete]"
   - Track TikTok is active and viral-friendly

### Success Metrics
- 500+ followers in first month
- Posts getting shared by featured athletes
- Organic downloads from social

---

## Phase 3: Community Outreach (Week 3-4)

### Goal
Get TrackHub in front of the broader track community.

### Actions
1. **Direct outreach to college track accounts**
   - DM team Instagram accounts (not official athletic dept)
   - Keep it casual: "Built this free app, your athletes are on it"
   - Don't spam - personalize each message

2. **Reddit**
   - Post in r/trackandfield
   - Be genuine: "I built this, looking for feedback"
   - Engage in comments, don't just drop link and leave

3. **Track Twitter/X**
   - Track Twitter is very active
   - Reply to track discussions
   - Share interesting stats from the app

4. **Track forums/communities**
   - LetsRun forums
   - Track-specific Discord servers
   - College track fan groups

### Success Metrics
- 500+ downloads
- Recognition in track community
- Organic mentions/shares

---

## Phase 4: Paid Growth (When Ready)

### Goal
Accelerate growth with targeted ads.

### When to Start
- After organic content is working
- After you have App Store reviews (social proof)
- When you have budget ($100-500 to test)

### Actions
1. **Instagram/Facebook ads**
   - Target: Track & field interest, ages 18-26
   - Creative: Show the app, feature real data
   - Start with $5-10/day to test
   - Scale what works

2. **TikTok ads**
   - Great for reaching younger athletes
   - Native-feeling content performs best
   - Test small before scaling

3. **Influencer partnerships**
   - Micro-influencers (5k-50k followers) in track
   - Offer free features or small payment
   - Authentic > polished
   - Track athletes with followings are ideal

### Success Metrics
- Cost per download under $1-2
- Downloads converting to active users

---

## Key Growth Tactics

### Make the App Shareable
- Athlete profile screenshots are inherently shareable
- Comparison results make great content
- "Share your WA score" feature (future)

### Leverage the Data
- You have valuable data that athletes want to see
- When athletes find themselves, they share
- Feature top performers = free marketing

### Timing
- Indoor season: Dec-Mar
- Outdoor season: Mar-Jun
- Championship season = peak interest
- Post content around major meets

### App Store Optimization
- Keywords: track, field, NCAA, TFRRS, college, athlete, PR
- Screenshots showing real data
- Reviews matter - ask happy users to review

## Metrics to Track

### Downloads
- Total downloads
- Downloads per week
- Source (organic vs paid vs referral)

### Engagement
- Daily active users
- Session length
- Most viewed features

### Social
- Follower count
- Post engagement rate
- Shares/mentions

### Retention
- Day 1, 7, 30 retention
- Users who return weekly
