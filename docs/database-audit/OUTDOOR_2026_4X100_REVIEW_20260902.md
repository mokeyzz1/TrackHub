## Safety and next gate

- No public result, relay, athlete, team, or meet row was changed by preparation or review.
- There is deliberately no public `apply` command.
- Production tracks 64 migrations; the five cleanup migrations applied directly are not yet
  represented in that history. Migration-history reconciliation must be planned separately and
  must not blindly re-run data migrations.

The next gate is manual/owner review of the 75 repair-ready plans and the 116 needs-review plans,
starting with unresolved teams and broken local team identities. Only after that review should a
separate, explicit public repair migration be designed.
