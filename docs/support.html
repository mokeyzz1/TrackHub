Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the data come from?

TrackHub displays publicly available track and field statistics from sources like TFRRS. We are not affiliated with any athletic governing body.

How do I delete my data?

All data is stored locally on your device. Simply uninstall the app to remove all data, or clear favorites within the app.

Is TrackHub free?

Yes, TrackHub is completely free to use with no ads or in-app purchases.

Can I request a feature?

Absolutely! Open an issue on our GitHub page or email us with your suggestions.
